Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This website (valerie-vienne.com) is the personal portfolio of Valerie Vienne. It is not a commercial service and has no user accounts. This page explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are sent to me by email so that I can read and reply to your message. I use this information only to respond to you. I do not sell it, share it with third parties for marketing, or add you to any mailing list.

Sending the email (Google user data)

The contact form delivers your message to my own Gmail inbox through the Gmail API. The site is authorised only to send emails to my own address. It does not read, store, or have access to the contents of my mailbox, and it does not access the mailbox of any visitor. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Spam protection (reCAPTCHA)

The contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to prevent spam. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information and passes it to Google for analysis, subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Analytics

I use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used (for example which pages are visited). It may set cookies and collect technical information such as your approximate location and browser type. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Theme preference

Your light/dark mode choice is stored in your browser (local storage) so the site remembers it. It is not sent to me.

Your rights and contact

You can ask me to access, correct or delete any message or personal data you sent me through the form. Please use the contact form to reach me. I keep contact messages only as long as needed to answer them.
